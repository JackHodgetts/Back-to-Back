You can see in this room a bed which is partitioned from the rest of the room with a curtain.
This is because the Oldfields also had two lodgers, who would have shared the bedroom with the Oldfields children.
It was common at this time for residents of Back to Back houses to take lodgers in to help them make ends meet and pay the rent. 
This means there may have been upwards of 11 people living in this house.

In the 1870s, the population of Birmingham had really grown. It was very common for families such as the Oldfields to take in lodgers so that they could afford to pay their rent.
