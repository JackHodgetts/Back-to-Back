On the bed, you can see some examples of the sorts of toys that children would have played with.
The Oldfields were a relatively poor family, so they only would've had toys that were handmade or hand-me-downs from other family members.
The doll on the bed is made from a shoe.
The skipping rope is also home-made.
And they would have had small toys, like peg soldiers and peg dolls that were easy to make with materials from home.
