# Back-to-Back Interactive Tour

An interactive touchscreen experience developed in collaboration with Birmingham's Back-to-Backs to provide visitors with an engaging and accessible digital tour of the historic site.

## Overview

The Back-to-Back Interactive Tour was a collaborative university project developed for Birmingham's Back-to-Backs.

The project was created to improve accessibility and allow visitors to explore historical content digitally. Due to the age and physical limitations of the historic buildings, not all areas are easily accessible to every visitor. The interactive tour provided an alternative way for visitors to engage with the site's history, stories, and media through a touchscreen experience.

The application was designed specifically for touchscreen hardware, with an iPad serving as the target deployment platform. Visitors could navigate through historical information, media, and interactive content in an intuitive and accessible format.

The project involved direct communication with stakeholders to gather requirements, receive feedback, and ensure the final product met the needs of both the organisation and its visitors.

## Challenges and Solutions

### Touchscreen User Experience

The application was designed specifically for touchscreen hardware, requiring interactions to be intuitive and accessible for a wide range of users.

This was addressed through the development and testing of touchscreen-focused interaction systems optimised for iPad devices.

### Managing Historical Content

A significant challenge was handling the large amount of historical information that needed to be displayed throughout the application while ensuring the content could be reviewed and updated efficiently.

The Back-to-Back stakeholders were very particular about the wording and presentation of historical information, which resulted in multiple rounds of feedback and revisions throughout development.

To support this process, text content was stored in external text files rather than being hardcoded directly into the application. This allowed content to be updated quickly without requiring extensive code changes and made it easier to incorporate stakeholder feedback as the project evolved.

### Collaborative Development

The project required coordination between team members while maintaining a stable and organised codebase.

GitHub was used to manage version control, coordinate development, and integrate contributions effectively throughout the project.
